# BeautyFlow — Marketing Plan

Supply first, then demand. A dish page with 15 places beats 3 counties with 2 each.

## 1. Seed supply by hand — first 100 vendors (weeks 1–4)
- Pick **2–3 dense areas** (Westlands, Kilimani, Ruaka / Nyali), not "all of Kenya".
- Walk in with a printed **QR card** and the menu-only page on your phone. Pitch: *"Free digital menu + WhatsApp orders in 10 minutes."* Set up the listing on the spot; take the photos yourself.
- Start with **kibandas and mid-range spots** (no website, instant value). Hotels come by reference later.
- Target: **5 sign-ups/day**. Track in Admin → Vendors.

## 2. Affiliate field force (month 2+)
- Recruit **boda riders, students, delivery riders** — people already inside restaurants.
- KES 20 per qualified sign-up; add a **KES 200–500 bonus when a referral buys Premium**.
- Give every affiliate the QR flyer with *their* link (auto-generated in their dashboard).
- **Monthly leaderboard**: top 3 win airtime / M-Pesa.

## 3. Every vendor is a channel
- QR card on tables and counters: *"Scan for menu · Powered by BeautyFlow."* Every scan = potential diner or new vendor.
- Ask each vendor to post their BeautyFlow link on **WhatsApp Status, Instagram bio, Google Business Profile** (share cards with preview image are ready).
- The "List your business" card at the bottom of every vendor page converts diners who own food businesses.

## 4. SEO (built — now feed it)
- Submit the sitemap in Search Console.
- Write **150–300 words of unique intro** for the top 10 location pages and 10 dish pages (Django admin → Locations / Tags → intro). This is what lifts "restaurants in Westlands" onto page 1.
- Publish **2 articles/month**: "10 best nyama choma spots in Nairobi", "Where to eat breakfast in Kisumu" — each linking to real vendor pages.
- Get **5–10 backlinks**: local blogs, Nairobi food Facebook groups, r/Kenya, university boards.

## 5. Social — one channel done well
- **TikTok / Reels**: 15-second "menu + price + location" clips of onboarded vendors, link in bio. Vendors reshare = free reach.
- **WhatsApp Channel "BeautyFlow Deals"** — weekly content straight from `/deals/`.
- Facebook groups (Nairobi Foodies, estate groups): post real deals, never ads.

## 6. Paid — only once organic works
- **Google Ads** on "list restaurant online Kenya", "digital menu Kenya" → `/for-business/…` landing pages. Small budget, high intent.
- Facebook/Instagram **lead ads** targeting restaurant owners in the seed areas.
- Sponsor **one university or estate food festival** — QR cards on every stall.

## 7. Monetisation pacing
- **Free for 3 months** in each new area. Push Premium/POS only once vendors see inquiries in their dashboard chart.
- Premium sells itself when the 10-item free cap bites.
- POS: pitch to vendors with ≥ 2 staff — waiter accounts and P&L are the hook.

## 8. Weekly numbers (all in admin)
Sign-ups · verified % · vendors with ≥ 5 items · inquiries per vendor · Premium conversions · affiliate qualified sign-ups · Search Console impressions for location pages.

## Next product levers
- Affiliate **Premium-conversion bonus**
- **Blog** section for the SEO articles
- **"Featured this week"** slot on the home page (sellable to vendors)
